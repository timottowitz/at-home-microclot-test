Microclot testing: what it costs and how to get it

Microclot testing is not a standard hospital test, so there is no single market price. One route has a public, fixed price. The others quote after an inquiry, bundle testing with a consultation, or enroll you in research. Everything below is a snapshot from October 2026.

Why the prices are hard to compare

Labs differ in sample type, how long a sample waits before processing, whether they use Thioflavin T fluorescence microscopy or imaging flow cytometry, and who reads the result. The useful comparison is the whole package: collection, shipping, processing, imaging, interpretation and follow-up.

Questions to ask before you pay

  1. Which sample do you use, and how is it transported?
  2. Which method do you use?
  3. Who reads the images?
  4. Does the report include raw images or video?
  5. Is the price itemized, and does it include follow-up?
  6. What can this test not tell me?
  7. Do you offer repeat testing?

No route here has a validated diagnostic cutoff. Results are observational data to review with your own clinician, not a diagnosis. Sudden leg swelling, chest pain or breathlessness needs urgent medical assessment.
